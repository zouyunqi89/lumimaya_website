Privacy Policy 隐私政策

Draft v1 — 2026-09-11. Adapted from the competitor reference template; to be reviewed and finalised before launch. 本政策为草稿，上线前需审核定稿。

1. Introduction 引言

Lumimaya ("we", "us") respects the privacy of every visitor and customer. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal data in accordance with the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). Lumimaya 尊重每位访客与顾客的隐私。本政策说明我们如何依据马来西亚《2010 年个人数据保护法》(PDPA) 收集、使用、披露和保护您的个人数据。

2. Personal data we collect 我们收集的个人数据

  • Identity data: name, date of birth. 身份信息：姓名、出生日期。
  • Contact data: email address, phone number (including WhatsApp). 联系信息：电子邮箱、电话号码（含 WhatsApp）。
  • Order data: shipping and billing addresses, items purchased, amounts. 订单信息：收货与账单地址、所购商品、金额。
  • Technical data: device, browser and usage information collected when you browse the site. 技术信息：浏览网站时收集的设备、浏览器及使用数据。

3. How we use your data 数据用途

  • To process and deliver your orders. 处理与配送订单。
  • To manage your account and provide customer support. 管理账户与提供客户服务。
  • With your consent, to send promotional emails about new products and offers. 经您同意后，发送新品与优惠信息。
  • To improve our website, products and services. 改进网站、产品与服务。

4. Disclosure 数据披露

We may share your data with trusted third parties only where necessary: delivery partners (to ship your order), payment providers (processed securely by third parties), and marketing platforms. We do not sell your personal data. 我们仅在必要时与可信第三方共享数据：物流合作伙伴（配送订单）、支付服务商（由第三方安全处理）及营销平台。我们不会出售您的个人数据。

5. Your rights 您的权利

  • To request access to, or correction of, your personal data. 请求查阅或更正您的个人数据。
  • To withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time (via the unsubscribe link or by contacting us). 随时撤回营销同意（通过邮件退订链接或联系我们）。
  • To request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal requirements. 在法律允许范围内请求删除个人数据。

6. Data protection 数据保护

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. 我们采取合理的技术与管理措施，保护您的个人数据免遭丢失、滥用和未经授权的访问。

7. Contact 联系我们

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at lumimayacollection@gmail.com or via WhatsApp. 如有任何隐私问题或请求，请通过上述邮箱或 WhatsApp 联系我们。

Terms and Conditions 条款与条件

  • Orders are confirmed personally via WhatsApp or email before payment. 订单在付款前会通过 WhatsApp 或邮件逐一确认。
  • Prices are shown in RM (Malaysian Ringgit); shipping is calculated per order and confirmed before payment. 价格以马币 (RM) 标示；运费按订单计算并在付款前确认。
  • Custom-made and personalised items are made to your confirmed measurements and cannot be exchanged except for defects. 定制商品按确认尺寸制作，除瑕疵外不支持换货。
  • See the Exchange & Return Policy for details on exchanges and returns. 退换货详情请参阅《退换货政策》。
