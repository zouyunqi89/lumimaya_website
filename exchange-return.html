Exchange & Return Policy 退换货政策

Draft v1 — 2026-09-11. Framework adapted from the Bayu Somerset reference; timelines and conditions to be confirmed before launch. 框架参考竞品，上线前需确认具体时间线与条件。

1. Exchange — size / style 换货（尺寸/款式）

  • Exchange for a different size or style is available within 7 days of delivery, provided the item is unworn, unwashed, with tags attached and in original packaging. 签收后 7 天内可申请更换尺码或款式，商品须未经穿着、未洗涤、吊牌完整并保持原包装。
  • To request an exchange, contact us on WhatsApp with your order number, item and the desired size/style. 换货请通过 WhatsApp 联系我们，提供订单号、商品及期望的尺码/款式。
  • We will confirm stock availability before approving the exchange. 我们会先确认库存再批准换货。
  • Return shipping for a change of mind (size/style) is borne by the customer; we cover shipping for our own errors. 因个人原因（尺码/款式）换货的运费由买家承担；因我方错误的运费由我们承担。
  • Custom-made / personalised items cannot be exchanged except for defects. 定制类商品除瑕疵外不支持换货。

2. Return — defective / wrongly shipped items 退货（瑕疵品/错发商品）

  • If your item arrives defective, damaged or wrongly shipped, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. 如商品存在瑕疵、损坏或错发，请在签收后 7 天内联系我们并提供商品与包装照片。
  • After verification, you may choose a replacement, an exchange, or a refund. 核实后您可选择补发、换货或退款。
  • Refunds are issued via the original payment method within 7–14 working days after we receive the returned item. 退款将于收到退货后 7–14 个工作日内按原付款方式退回。
  • Return shipping for defective or wrongly shipped items is borne by Lumimaya. 瑕疵品/错发商品的退货运费由 Lumimaya 承担。

3. Conditions & exclusions 条件与例外

  • Items must be returned in original condition; worn, washed, altered or fragrance-affected items are not eligible. 退回商品须保持原状；已穿着、洗涤、修改或附着异味的商品不予受理。
  • Sale/clearance items, fabric swatches and accessories (e.g. tudung worn for hygiene reasons) are final sale unless defective. 特价商品、布料小样及出于卫生原因佩戴过的头巾等配饰，除瑕疵外不退不换。
  • Approved exchanges/returns are processed after we receive and inspect the returned item. 换货/退货在收到并检查退回商品后处理。

4. How to start 如何申请

WhatsApp us at +60 12-345 6789 or email lumimayacollection@gmail.com with your order number and photos (for defects). We usually reply within 1 working day. 请通过 WhatsApp 或邮件联系我们，附上订单号及（如为瑕疵）照片，我们通常于 1 个工作日内回复。
