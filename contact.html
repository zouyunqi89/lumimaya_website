Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order? ▾

Add products to your cart and check out via WhatsApp, or leave us a message below — we will confirm details, price and delivery with you personally.

Shipping fee ▾

Shipping within Malaysia is calculated by weight and destination; we confirm the exact fee with your order on WhatsApp. Free shipping promotions are announced on our social media.

Returns & exchanges ▾

Size/style exchanges and returns for defective or wrongly-shipped items are supported within 7 days of delivery — see our Exchange & Return policy page for details.

Other services ▾

Custom design, small-batch production, packaging services and label making are all available. Tell us what you need.

Packaging service ▾

Gift wrapping and branded packaging options are available for orders — mention it when you check out.

Label making ▾

We offer custom woven and printed labels for your own brand — minimum order quantities apply, contact us for a quote.
